Featured Editorial · LFJ-001

The Architecture of
Transition Intelligence

Exploring a computational approach to continuity, transition-state awareness and the geometry of systems in motion.

EDITORIAL · 31 AUGUST 2026
Featured This Month

Our editorial team was pleased to welcome Anthea Tenayiah Leia Govender as the principal feature of our inaugural issue.

An AI Systems Architect, independent researcher and founder of the ENNORY Intelligence Platform, Govender is working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, geometry, continuity and emerging computational systems.

Our conversation moves beyond the familiar language of artificial intelligence to examine a more intriguing question: what happens inside a system while everything is changing?

About the Journal

A forum for emerging systems thinking.

London Forum Journal is an independent editorial publication focused on ideas emerging across technology, computational systems, innovation and interdisciplinary research.

Editorial Position

The journal presents original perspectives, technical concepts and emerging frameworks while distinguishing editorial publication from formal peer review or institutional accreditation.

Publication does not by itself constitute independent scientific validation. Readers are encouraged to examine the underlying work, evidence and references associated with each article.
